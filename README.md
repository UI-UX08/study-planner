# Adaptive Study Planner Mobile App

## Project Title
**Adaptive Study Planner Mobile App**

## Problem Statement
Many students struggle to effectively organize their academic workload due to multiple courses, assignments, exams, and deadlines. Traditional planners and simple to-do list applications often lack intelligent scheduling, progress monitoring, and adaptive features that respond to changing academic demands. This results in poor time management, missed deadlines, increased stress, and reduced academic performance. The Adaptive Study Planner Mobile App addresses these challenges by providing a personalized and interactive platform that helps students plan, manage, and monitor their studies efficiently.

## Project Objectives

### General Objective
To design and develop a mobile application that assists students in planning, organizing, and tracking their academic activities through an adaptive and user-friendly interface.

### Specific Objectives
- Enable students to add and manage courses.
- Allow users to create and organize academic tasks.
- Generate and manage personalized study schedules.
- Allow users to mark completed tasks and monitor progress.
- Automatically reschedule missed tasks.
- Provide reminders and notifications for upcoming deadlines.
- Display dashboards that visualize study progress and productivity.
- Improve students' time management and study habits through adaptive interaction.

## Target Users
The application is intended for:
- University students
- College students
- High school students
- Online learners
- Anyone seeking an organized approach to study planning and task management

## Main Features

### Course Management
- Add, edit, and delete courses.
- Organize courses by semester or category.

### Academic Task Management
- Create assignments, quizzes, projects, and exam reminders.
- Set deadlines and priorities.
- Edit or remove tasks.

### Study Schedule
- Create personalized study timetables.
- Allocate study sessions based on available time.
- View daily and weekly schedules.

### Progress Tracking
- Mark tasks as completed.
- Monitor study completion rates.
- View progress through charts and dashboards.

### Adaptive Rescheduling
- Detect missed tasks.
- Automatically suggest new study times.
- Help maintain realistic study plans.

### Notifications and Reminders
- Receive reminders for upcoming tasks.
- Get alerts for missed deadlines.
- Receive motivational feedback after completing study goals.

### Dashboard
- View overall academic progress.
- Track completed and pending tasks.
- Monitor study streaks and productivity statistics.

## Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) Focus

The application emphasizes the following HCI principles:

- **Personalization**
  - Personalized study schedules based on user preferences.
  - Custom reminders and notification settings.

- **Adaptive Interaction**
  - Automatically adjusts schedules when tasks are missed.
  - Suggests optimal study times.

- **Dashboards**
  - Visual summaries of progress and productivity.
  - Easy-to-understand charts and statistics.

- **Notifications**
  - Timely reminders for deadlines and study sessions.
  - Adaptive notifications based on user activity.

- **Feedback**
  - Immediate confirmation after completing tasks.
  - Progress indicators and motivational messages.

- **User Control**
  - Users can edit schedules and tasks at any time.
  - Manual override of automatically generated study plans.

## Project Summary

The Adaptive Study Planner Mobile App is a student-centered productivity application designed to improve academic organization and time management. The application enables students to manage courses, create academic tasks, develop personalized study schedules, track learning progress, and receive intelligent reminders. By incorporating adaptive scheduling, interactive dashboards, and user-focused design principles, the app enhances the study experience while promoting better planning, accountability, and academic success.
